Bienvenidos a nuestra casa

Two roots. A whole lot of flavor.

Casa Yari is chef Yari Vargas's corner of Humboldt Park, where Puerto Rican and Honduran family recipes share one kitchen. Sweet plantain and slow-cooked guiso sit next to a vegan menu she taught herself to cook — plus the flan everybody talks about.

Nuestra historia

A Puerto Rican mom from Lares. A Honduran dad from Progreso. One Chicago kitchen.

Yari Vargas grew up cooking in Humboldt Park, in a house where Puerto Rico and Honduras both showed up at dinner. She opened Casa Yari in 2013 to serve those family plates the way she remembers them — then taught herself vegan and vegetarian cooking so that everybody at the table gets something made with the same care.

“I tell my story through food.”

— Chef Yari Vargas, owner & executive chef

Puerto Rico × Honduras

Two islands, one table.

Yari's mother is from Lares, Puerto Rico. Her father is an Afro-Latino from Progreso, Honduras. Both showed up at dinner — so both show up on the menu, and neither one is playing a supporting role.

🇵🇷

Puerto Rico

from Lares

Sofrito, sweet plantain and slow-braised pork — the cooking Yari grew up with in Humboldt Park, made the long way round.

  • Pastelón (piñón)Sweet plantain, cheese, criollo sauce — picadillo or pernil.
  • Mofongo of the monthA rotating mofongo. Call us to hear what the month is.
  • La caribeñaShrimp in tomatina criollo coconut sauce on mashed malanga lila.
  • Mami's vegan bowlHer mom's favourites: rice, stewed beans with pumpkin, fried cauliflower.

Honduras

from Progreso

Her father's side of the kitchen: catracho comfort food built on green banana, azafrán and chimol, with the crunch of a proper tajada.

  • Pollo chucoFried chicken thigh, green banana tajadas, cabbage, tomatina, pink aioli.
  • Marmahón de polloGrilled chicken in Honduran azafrán and house dry sofrito rub.
  • DobladasHonduran crispy tacos with chicken, red cabbage, queso fresco, spicy aioli.
  • Plátano catrachoSweet plantain topped with crema and queso. Vegan option available.

and where they meet

The dishes where the two kitchens borrow from each other — guanime con bacalao wrapped in banana leaves, pescado en escabeche over smashed malanga, and the jibarito that Eater Chicago put on its list of the best in the city.

Hecho a mano, sin carne

A full vegan menu, taught herself.

Nobody handed Yari a plant-based playbook. She taught herself vegan and vegetarian cooking so that every person at the table gets a plate made with the same care — not a side salad with the meat taken off. Seitan, jackfruit, oyster mushrooms and soy curls, cooked with the same sofrito and the same patience.

  • 🌱Marked on the menu with a leaf
  • 🌱Full sections of their own — not substitutions
  • 🌱Milk or oat milk for the summer milkshakes
See the vegan menu↗

From the vegan side

  • 🌱 Pastelón vegano Sweet plantain layered with seitan picadillo and vegan cheese, sundried tomato and onion compote, fresh dill.
  • 🌱 Vegan mofongo de ropa vieja Double-fried green plantain volcano cup filled with shredded jackfruit ropa vieja.
  • 🌱 Vegan jackfruit paella Roasted jackfruit in a veggie paella mix, red chimichurri, lemon, sweet plantain.
  • 🌱 Mushrooms & tajadas (chuco) Oyster mushrooms done Honduran fried-chicken style over green banana, tomatina and chimol.
  • 🌱 Alitas de coliflor Breaded cauliflower wings with guava BBQ.
  • 🌱 Mami's vegan bowl White rice, stewed red beans with pumpkin, red cabbage, fried cauliflower, pink aioli.

Vegan pinchos, soy-dobladas and a vegan pastelón round it out — ask your server what's on tonight.

Los favoritos de la casa

The plates people cross the city for.

Family recipes, elevated — and never standing still. Some nights it's sweet plantain layered with slow-cooked beef. Some nights it's a mofongo you have to call about, because the rotation changes with the month.

Ask for these

  • 🌱 Alitas de coliflor Breaded cauliflower wings with guava BBQ.
